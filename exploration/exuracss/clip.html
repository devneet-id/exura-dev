Clip

Emoji icons as an attribute: clip="e:NAME a:ANIMATION ef:EFFECT". The old forms clip="NAME", animate:NAME and effect:NAME are marked for deprecation.

animation — a:*

On e:rocket.

<i clip="e:rocket a:bounce"></i>

effect — ef:*

On e:star.

<i clip="e:star ef:ghost"></i>
