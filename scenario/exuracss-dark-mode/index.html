Dark mode by reversal

dark on any element swaps light ↔ dark and white ↔ black (with shades); light inside brings the normal pair back.

  • the left panel is light, the right panel is dark
  • inside the dark panel, the nested [light] box is light again
  • buttons dark and light swap colors between the panels
  • text stays readable everywhere
