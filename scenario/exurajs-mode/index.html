Switch mode: development & production

Exura.configure({ mode }) rebases the settings of exurajs. development (the default) prints a banner, tracks every module, and warns about clashing names and missing elements. production prints nothing and catches every error — in an effect, a timer or a promise — and hands it to onError. The page runs development first, then switches to production.
